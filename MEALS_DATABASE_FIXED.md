## 📐 CALORIE CALCULATION FORMULA

All calories are calculated correctly using:

**Calories = (Protein × 4) + (Carbs × 4) + (Fat × 9)**

Example:
- Protein: 40g × 4 = 160 kcal
- Carbs: 50g × 4 = 200 kcal
- Fat: 12g × 9 = 108 kcal
- **Total: 468 kcal** ✅
